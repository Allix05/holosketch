Draw It. Hold It.

Pinch your thumb and index finger to sketch a shape in the air, then throw a shaka (🤙 thumb + pinky) and it becomes a real 3D hologram — floating right between your thumb and middle finger like you're actually holding it. Move your hand to carry it, turn your wrist to spin it, and squeeze your fingers together or apart to shrink or grow it. Runs entirely in your browser, no server, video never leaves your device.

Needs camera access. Everything runs locally — nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  • Draw — MediaPipe's Hand Landmarker tracks your hand at 21 points; pinching thumb + index draws a stroke on an invisible plane in front of the camera.
  • Shaka (🤙) — raise thumb + pinky and the stroke is simplified into a clean polygon (Ramer–Douglas–Peucker), closed if needed, and extruded into real 3D geometry with Three.js/WebGL — a genuine solid, not a flat sprite — with a materializing flash as it snaps into being.
  • Hold — no separate grab gesture: every frame, the hologram's position and spin are driven live by the point between your thumb and middle finger and your wrist rotation, while its size tracks the live gap between those same two fingers — squeeze them together and it shrinks with them, so it never visually pokes through. Everything is smoothed frame to frame so it settles like a real object instead of jittering with tracking noise.
