Draw It. Hold It.

Pinch your thumb and index finger to sketch a shape in the air, then raise your pinky and it becomes a real 3D hologram — resting right in your open hand. Move your hand to carry it, turn your wrist to spin it, and bring your hand closer or farther from the camera to make it bigger or smaller. Runs entirely in your browser, no server, video never leaves your device.

Needs camera access. Everything runs locally — nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  • Draw — MediaPipe's Hand Landmarker tracks your hand at 21 points; pinching thumb + index draws a stroke on an invisible plane in front of the camera.
  • Raise your pinky — the stroke is simplified into a clean polygon (Ramer–Douglas–Peucker), closed if needed, and extruded into real 3D geometry with Three.js/WebGL — a genuine solid, not a flat sprite.
  • Hold — no separate grab gesture: every frame, the hologram's position, spin, and size are driven live by your open hand's palm position, wrist rotation, and distance from the camera — like a real object resting in your hand.
